About Us

A Place Where Every Journey Matters

M.B. Therapy Center is a multi-disciplinary therapy and special education center in Taguig City dedicated to providing individualized, compassionate care to children and individuals with special needs.

Our licensed therapists and special educators collaborate to create personalized plans that help each person discover and reach their full potential.

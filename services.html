Special Education Program

Special Education (SpED)

Our Special Education Program is designed for individuals with developmental disabilities, learning difficulties, and cognitive challenges. Our certified Special Education teachers develop highly individualized educational plans (IEPs) that address each learner's unique strengths, needs, and learning style.

We offer both One-on-One Sessions — for focused, individualized instruction — and Group Sessions that foster social skills, peer interaction, and collaborative learning in a structured environment.

1-on-1 Sessions

Personalized instruction focused on individual goals, at the learner's own pace

Group Sessions

Social skills development and peer-based learning in small, structured groups

OT Program

Occupational Therapy (OT)

Occupational therapy helps individuals develop, maintain, or regain the skills needed for daily living and meaningful activities. Our OT specialists use purposeful activities to improve physical, cognitive, and social skills, enabling greater independence.

We address fine motor skills, sensory processing, self-care routines, handwriting, play skills, and school readiness — creating a strong foundation for life at home, school, and in the community.

Fine and gross motor skill development

Sensory processing and integration therapy

Activities of daily living (ADL) training

Handwriting and pre-writing skill development

Play skills and social participation

ST Program

Speech Therapy (ST)

Our Speech-Language Pathologists work with individuals experiencing communication challenges, speech disorders, language delays, and feeding/swallowing difficulties. Communication is a fundamental right, and we are here to help every individual find their voice.

We use a range of evidence-based approaches including play-based therapy, augmentative and alternative communication (AAC), oral motor therapy, and structured language programs to help individuals communicate effectively and confidently.

PT Program

Physical Therapy (PT)

Our licensed Physical Therapists focus on improving mobility, strength, balance, and overall physical function. We provide targeted rehabilitation to help individuals with motor disorders, musculoskeletal conditions, neurological conditions, and developmental delays achieve greater physical independence.

From therapeutic exercises and manual therapy to hydrotherapy and developmental play, our PT team designs safe, progressive programs that help patients regain confidence in their physical abilities.

Gross motor development and movement rehabilitation

Balance, coordination, and posture training

Cerebral palsy and neurological rehabilitation

Therapeutic exercise and strength building

Tutorial Services

Tutorial Services

Our Tutorial Services provide personalized academic support for students with learning disabilities, attention difficulties, and other conditions that affect academic performance. Our tutors work closely with each student's school curriculum while adapting instruction to meet the student's learning needs.

Whether your child needs help catching up in core subjects, building foundational reading and math skills, or developing effective study habits, our team provides patient, effective support in a comfortable, encouraging environment.

How It Works

Starting Your Therapy Journey

1

Initial Inquiry

Contact us by phone, email, or our online form. We'll answer your questions and help identify which service is right for you.

2

Assessment

A comprehensive evaluation by our specialist to understand strengths, challenges, and goals — the foundation for your personalized plan.

3

Therapy Plan

We design a customized therapy plan with clear objectives, timelines, and measurable goals, reviewed regularly with you and your family.

4

Progress & Growth

Regular sessions, progress monitoring, and family updates ensure we celebrate every milestone and adjust the plan as needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services, scheduling, eligibility, and payments.

General Questions

M.B. Therapy Center is a multi-disciplinary therapy and special education center located in Taguig City. We provide individualized, evidence-based therapy services including Special Education, Occupational Therapy, Speech Therapy, Physical Therapy, and Tutorial Services to children and individuals with special needs and disabilities.

We serve individuals across a wide range of ages, from toddlers in early intervention programs to school-age children and teenagers. We also provide services for adults when appropriate. Early intervention is especially important and we welcome families as soon as a concern or diagnosis arises.

A doctor's referral is not always required, but it is helpful. For some services, especially Physical Therapy and Occupational Therapy, a referral from a pediatrician or developmental pediatrician helps us understand your child's medical history and tailor the program accordingly. Feel free to contact us and we'll guide you through the process.

Yes. All our therapists are fully licensed professionals in their respective fields — including Registered Occupational Therapists (OTR), Registered Physical Therapists (RPT), Registered Speech-Language Pathologists (RSLP), and Licensed Professional Teachers (LPT) with Special Education certification. We require all team members to maintain active licenses and pursue continuing education.

About Our Services

A typical individual therapy session lasts 45–60 minutes. Group sessions may run slightly longer. The frequency and duration of sessions vary depending on the type of therapy and the individual's needs and goals, as determined during the initial assessment.

Yes. Many of our patients benefit from receiving multiple therapies simultaneously — for example, SpED with Speech Therapy, or OT with Physical Therapy. Our multi-disciplinary team collaborates closely to ensure all therapies work together in a coordinated, complementary approach tailored to your child's goals.

We work with individuals with a wide range of conditions including Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD), Down Syndrome, Cerebral Palsy, ADHD/ADD, Intellectual Disabilities, Learning Disabilities (dyslexia, dyscalculia), Global Developmental Delay, Language and Speech Delays, Sensory Processing Disorders, and more. If you're unsure whether your child's condition is within our scope, please contact us — we're happy to discuss.

Contact us for a free consultation. Our team will discuss your child's challenges and goals and recommend the most appropriate services. For definitive recommendations, we conduct a comprehensive initial assessment to fully understand your child's strengths and areas for growth before designing a therapy plan.

Scheduling & Appointments

We are open Monday through Saturday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. We are closed on Sundays and Philippine public holidays. For urgent inquiries outside office hours, you may send us a message on Facebook or email us and we will respond the next business day.

You can book an appointment by: (1) filling out our online booking form on this website, (2) calling us at (02) 8511-7239 or 0933-720-2394, or (3) sending us a message on our Facebook page. We will confirm your appointment and send you the details for your first visit.

Please bring: valid government ID of the parent/guardian, the child's birth certificate, any medical or psychological evaluation reports you may have, any previous therapy records or IEPs (Individualized Education Programs), doctor's referral (if available), and any other relevant medical history documents. Having these documents helps us create the most effective therapy plan from the start.

Yes, you may reschedule or cancel appointments. We ask that you inform us at least 24 hours in advance so that we can accommodate other patients in need. Repeated late cancellations or no-shows may affect your scheduling priority. Please call us or send a message at the contact information provided.

Payments & Fees

Session fees vary depending on the type of therapy, session duration, and whether it is a one-on-one or group session. Please contact us directly for a current fee schedule. We strive to keep our services as accessible and affordable as possible for families.

We accept cash payments at our center. We also accept bank transfers and GCash payments. Please ask our staff for our payment details when you book your appointment or arrive for your session. Receipts are issued for all payments.

Please contact us directly to inquire about PhilHealth and HMO coverage, as this may vary depending on the specific service and coverage plan. We are happy to provide any documentation needed to support a claim with your insurance provider.

Our Mission

Empowering Every Individual

To provide high-quality, individualized, and evidence-based therapy and special education services that empower children and individuals with disabilities to achieve their fullest potential, fostering independence, confidence, and a meaningful quality of life.

We are committed to partnering with families and caregivers to ensure that every person in our care feels seen, valued, and supported throughout their journey.

Our Vision

A World of Possibilities

To be the leading multi-disciplinary therapy and special education center in Taguig City and the National Capital Region — recognized for our excellence in care, innovation in treatment, and unwavering commitment to inclusion and accessibility for all.

We envision a community where every individual, regardless of ability, has access to the support they need to live fully and independently.

Our Story

How M.B. Therapy Center Began

M.B. Therapy Center was founded with a profound belief: that every person, regardless of their challenges, deserves the opportunity to grow, learn, and thrive. Our founders, driven by personal experiences with family members who needed specialized care, recognized a gap in accessible, high-quality therapy services in Taguig City.

Starting with just a small team of dedicated therapists, M.B. Therapy Center grew through word of mouth — families sharing stories of progress, milestones reached, and lives transformed. Today, we are proud to serve hundreds of children and individuals across the metro.

Our tagline, "From Disabilities to Possibilities," is not just a motto — it's a promise we renew with every session, every breakthrough, and every smile we witness.

Core Values

What We Stand For

These values are the foundation of every interaction, every treatment plan, and every relationship we build.

Compassion

We approach every patient and family with empathy, kindness, and genuine care. Every person who walks through our doors is treated with dignity and respect.

Excellence

We maintain the highest standards in therapy and education through continuous professional development, evidence-based practices, and personalized care planning.

Inclusion

We celebrate diversity and believe that every individual deserves equal access to therapy and education, regardless of background, ability, or circumstance.

Collaboration

Our multi-disciplinary team works together with families, caregivers, and schools to provide a holistic, coordinated approach to each individual's care.

Integrity

We act with transparency, honesty, and ethical responsibility in all aspects of our work — from treatment recommendations to billing and communication.

Growth

We believe in the limitless potential of every individual. We are committed to celebrating every milestone and nurturing continuous progress, no matter how small.
